Here is the physics. When water flows and hits a solid object, the object stops the water, but the water keeps pushing. Anything caught between the two takes the full force of everything upstream.

And the force scales viciously. Double the speed of the water and the push goes up roughly four times. That's why a current that feels manageable at walking pace becomes immovable when it picks up slightly.

So a person pinned against a fence is not simply standing in water. They are being held there by pressure that increases the more of their body is in the flow. They usually cannot push themselves off it, because any movement that exposes more surface area makes the push stronger.
